Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) & Farm Machinery Subsidies

Government Scheme Overview:
The Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) is a major government scheme launched by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, to promote farm mechanization, increase crop productivity, and reduce operational costs for farmers across India.

Key Objectives:
1. Increasing reaching of farm mechanization to small and marginal farmers and to regions & states where availability of farm power is low.
2. Promoting 'Custom Hiring Centres' (CHC) to offset the high cost of purchasing individual high-value farm equipment and machinery.
3. Creating hubs for hi-tech & high-value farm equipment.
4. Assisting farmers with financial subsidies for purchasing modern agricultural machinery, implements, and machinery tools.

Financial Subsidies & Benefits:
- Small, Marginal, Women, and SC/ST Farmers: Financial assistance of up to 50% subsidy on purchase of farm machinery, tractors, power tillers, rotavators, seed drills, multi-crop threshers, combines, and plant protection equipment.
- General Category Farmers: Financial assistance of up to 40% subsidy on eligible farm equipment.
- Custom Hiring Centres (CHC): Grants up to 40% of the project cost (maximum assistance up to Rs. 10 Lakhs to Rs. 24 Lakhs) for establishing farm equipment custom hiring centres.
- Farm Machinery Banks: Financial assistance of up to 80% (up to Rs. 10 Lakhs) for Cooperative Societies, Self Help Groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).

Eligible Machinery & Tools:
1. Tractors, Mini Tractors, and Power Tillers.
2. Land Preparation Machinery: Laser land levellers, ploughs, harrows, cultivators, rotavators.
3. Sowing and Planting Machinery: Seed-cum-fertilizer drills, happy seeders, super seeders, zero-till drills, paddy transplanters.
4. Harvesting Machinery: Combine harvesters, reapers, balers, multi-crop threshers.
5. Plant Protection Equipment: Power sprayers, tractor-mounted sprayers, agricultural drones.

Eligibility Criteria:
- Indian farmers, small and marginal agricultural landholders.
- Self Help Groups (SHGs), Cooperative Societies, Registered Farmer Groups, and FPOs.
- Applicants must possess valid land records (Pahani/RTC/7/12 extract), Aadhaar Card, Bank Account details, and Caste Certificate (if applicable).

Application Process:
- Farmers can apply online through the official Direct Benefit Transfer in Agricultural Mechanization portal (agrimachinery.nic.in) or state agriculture portal.
- Subsidies are transferred directly to the farmer's verified bank account (DBT mode).
